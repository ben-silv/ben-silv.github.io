01 — Research

Immunophenotyping, one cell at a time.

Mass cytometry measures fifty-plus protein markers on a single cell. Collecting the data is the easy part — the work is building a pipeline where the populations you call at the end still mean something biologically.

Undergraduate Researcher, Vaccine & Immunotherapy Center

Massachusetts General Hospital, Boston

  • Engineered a Python pipeline that profiles immune cell populations across vaccine stimulations, on datasets past a million events.
  • Wrote the preprocessing end to end: FCS parsing, arcsinh transformation, dimensionality reduction with PCA and UMAP.
  • Augmented the existing clustering with biological validation metrics, so population assignments hold up to immunological interpretation instead of just looking tidy.
