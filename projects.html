Projects

Both of these started because someone I know had a problem that software could plausibly help with, and neither of them is a demo.

CraveCast

September 2026

People in recovery get a warning before a craving lands rather than a record of it afterwards, forecast from their own patterns and the peer-reviewed ADARP study.

It runs entirely in the browser and works with the network off, so nothing about somebody's recovery ever leaves their device. That constraint shaped most of the architecture.

Status

EpiAlert

May 2026

A photograph of a meal becomes an allergen decision in seconds, with the nearest stocked epinephrine on the same screen.

Built for travelling with a severe allergy, where knowing you are in trouble is only half the problem. I cut a peer-review feed late on once it was clear crowd-sourced safety data is a liability when the failure mode is anaphylaxis.

Built with

Python, Flask, Claude Vision API, Google Maps API
