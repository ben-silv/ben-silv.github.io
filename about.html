03 — About

Two tracks, one question.

I came to Tufts for computer science and stayed for the biology. The lab work gave me the vocabulary; CS gave me the tools. Somewhere in the overlap I found the thing I actually want to do — turning high-dimensional immune data into something a clinician can act on.

Before the VIC I was at Sonkusale Research Labs fabricating flexible electrode arrays, which taught me that a measurement is only as good as the thing taking it. That lesson transfers: half of mass cytometry is trusting your panel, and half of research computing is making sure the infrastructure never becomes the reason an experiment stalls.
